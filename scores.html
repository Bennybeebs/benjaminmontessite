Film & Media

Scoring Work

Benjamin Montes works primarily as a film and media composer, scoring short films, feature documentaries, and commercial projects. His concert and album releases exist alongside — and often in direct dialogue with — his work for picture. He currently studies Media Scoring and Production at the Frost School of Music at the University of Miami.
